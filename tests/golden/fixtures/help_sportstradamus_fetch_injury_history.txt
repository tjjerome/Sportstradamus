Usage: sportstradamus fetch injury-history [OPTIONS]

  Pull every league's injury history, one parquet per season.

  NFL injury reports, MLB injured-list moves and ESPN's NHL injured-reserve
  moves are re-pulled whole; the NBA, WNBA and NHL per-game absence lists fetch
  only games not yet stored. The NHL injured-reserve file for a season holds
  that calendar year.

  The NFL default stops at nflreadpy's current season, not the calendar year:
  nflverse has no file for a season until its injury reports start in September.

Options:
  --league [nfl|mlb|nba|wnba|nhl]
                                  League to pull; repeatable. Defaults to every
                                  league.
  --season INTEGER                Season to pull; repeatable. Defaults to 2022
                                  through the current one.
  --help                          Show this message and exit.
