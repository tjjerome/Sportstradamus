Usage: sportstradamus meditate [OPTIONS]

  Train or retrain LightGBMLSS models for each configured market.

Options:
  --force / --no-force            Skip markets with fresh data (default). Pass
                                  --force to rebuild the prior correlation CSV
                                  cache and build markets whose models are
                                  already up to date.
  --league [All|NFL|NBA|MLB|NHL|WNBA]
                                  Select league to train on
  --rebuild-correlations / --no-rebuild-correlations
                                  Rebuild only the per-league correlation
                                  matrices and exit; skip the per-market
                                  training loop
  --log-level [DEBUG|INFO|WARNING|ERROR]
                                  Verbosity for the structured JSONL log.
  --market TEXT                   Comma-separated market stem(s) to train (e.g.
                                  'FTM,STL'). When omitted, all markets for the
                                  active league are trained. A stem absent from
                                  every active league is an error.
  --branch [devel|main]           Which release branch's ship policy to honor.
                                  'devel' (default, matches the production
                                  server) trains every cell with shipped in
                                  {devel, main}. 'main' only trains cells with
                                  shipped=main. See data/config/stat_meta.json.
                                  [default: devel]
  --matrix-only / --no-matrix-only
                                  Assemble and persist each cell's training
                                  matrix (and comps), then stop before
                                  distribution selection and model training.
                                  Warms data/training_data/ without training;
                                  pair with --bypass-withholding to reach
                                  withheld cells.
  --help                          Show this message and exit.
