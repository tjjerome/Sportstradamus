Usage: sportstradamus confer [OPTIONS]

  Fetch current odds and player props into the archive.

Options:
  --close-lines                   Targeted close-line scrape only. Fetches games
                                  in data/runtime/upcoming_events.json starting
                                  within the closing window into
                                  data/runtime/close_line_captures.json, then
                                  archives that spool once the archive lock
                                  frees, else leaves it to a later run. Exits
                                  without touching API, lock or archive when
                                  nothing is due or spooled.
  --fixture-dir DIRECTORY         Read canned Odds API responses from this
                                  directory instead of hitting the live API.
                                  Used by the integration test suite. Skips
                                  game-level moneyline fetches; player props are
                                  loaded from <fixture_dir>/sports.json,
                                  <fixture_dir>/events_<sport_key>.json, and <fi
                                  xture_dir>/odds_<sport_key>_<event_id>.json.
  --log-level [DEBUG|INFO|WARNING|ERROR]
                                  Verbosity for the structured JSONL log.
  --help                          Show this message and exit.
