Usage: sportstradamus fetch weather-history [OPTIONS]

  Store the archived pre-game forecast of NFL and MLB games already played.

Options:
  --league [nfl|mlb]  League to backfill; repeatable. Defaults to both.
  --season INTEGER    Season to backfill; repeatable. Defaults to 2022 through
                      the current year.
  --max-cost FLOAT    Stop once the run has spent this many Open-Meteo calls.
                      [default: 8000.0]
  --dry-run           Print the plan and its cost; request nothing from Open-
                      Meteo.
  --help              Show this message and exit.
